# Friction

In today's world there's never been more opportunities to not be bored. I actually wanted to name this blog post something about boredom or how the human mind ever since the invention
of a screen has increasingly higher stimulation needs to not be bored. Boredom is seen as a problem, something that must be fixed. The general response to a child saying "I'm bored."
is usually saying "Find something to do kid." and off he goes. Boredom rarely ever is a condition in which you actually have nothing you could be doing. Instead boredom is the disposition
in which we place ourselves when there's stuff we could be doing but we don't because we'd rather be stimulated. The amount of stimulation options we have these days has grown so much that
people on Youtube have came up with strategies to fix your brain from the damage a screen does. To rewire your mind to actually see difficult tasks clearly for what they are and execute. We could
talk all day about the brain chemistry behind these screens, the dopamine hits, serotonin, and of course cortisol the stress hormone. I just want to deliver a solution that I think works.

## Adding friction...

Friction is what stops you from sitting at you're job and pulling out your phone for a text message which inevitably leads to scrolling social media. The fix in an ideal world is to place
your phone out of reach and out of the room. You of course might need your phone to access your 2FA for the tools you use. The solution is a piece of tech that puts massive friction between
social media. In my case a smart watch. The analogy I want to focus on is cloud computing. The cloud is your person, the compute is your mobile device and the watch is the interface from which
all this productivity can be achieved.
