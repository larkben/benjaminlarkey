AI now known as SI is changing. Not only has the technology been renamed to reflect where these models are going; respectively Artificial Intelligence implying something was fake now Super Intelligence. Today we face three types of people... those who will use SI in synergy with their workloads and produce an even better result. Those who use SI and get lazy... in which rather than the user controlling the SI the SI contorls the user. The last of which is the people who refuse to use SI altogether. Refusing to use SI I believe is a more costly risk than using it and not knowing how to use it properly. Today's models will extract data from users and will even steal ideas. Owning the models, but more specifically the infrastructure has never been more important. Compute prices have soared, the demand for access to more and more powerful frontier models has skyrocketed and with it the demand for private models and or compute. The future will belong to those with the infrastructure to run AGI locally.
